  .venv/bin/python3 run.py refresh --no-open

Re-render without publishing:
  .venv/bin/python3 run.py open --no-open

After the separate ChatGPT Work task imports current employer-JD evidence, create the
initial baseline once with `run.py baseline --no-open`; use `run.py publish --no-open`
for later new-only daily reports.

Target: requested analyst/product titles, explicitly US eligible, 2–5 required years,
full-time employee, current JD within 30 days, and USD annual base reaching $100,000.
Crossing ranges are cautions. Sponsorship silence is “Not stated / needs confirmation.”

The tracker never applies or contacts anyone.
